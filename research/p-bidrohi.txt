 Bidrohi V2 Rocket, developed by Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Limited (DhumketuX), is Bangladesh’s first commercial sub-orbital space rocket, designed to reach sub-orbital space and propel the nation into the global aerospace industry. Named after the iconic poem “Bidrohi” by Kazi Nazrul Islam, the rocket symbolizes the rebellious spirit, resilience, and rising ambition of Bangladesh.

 Bidrohi Rocket — V1 & V2
 The Bidrohi Rocket Program by DhumketuX is being developed in two versions for Bangladesh’s suborbital space missions.
Bidrohi V1 is a dedicated Kármán Line test vehicle, designed primarily to reach approximately 100 km altitude with a limited payload. Its mission is to validate propulsion, flight control, telemetry, recovery, and overall suborbital flight performance.
Bidrohi V2 is the main operational version, designed for higher payload capacity and future scientific, university, technology-demonstration, and commercial suborbital missions.
Development Path: Bidrohi V1 Flight Test → Flight Data Validation → System Improvement → Bidrohi V2 Operational Missions.
 Tentative Launch Date: 2027
