The First Rocket Putimach 01 (পুটিমাছ ০১)
DhumketuX, the first aerospace research & development lab of Bangladesh, successfully launches it’s first ever prototype sounding rocket called “Putimach 01 (পুটিমাছ ০১)” by DhumketuX junior’s team. This is the first time in the history of Bangladesh to launch any kinds of ROCKETS!
Rocket Putimach 01 launch event
Bangladesh’s first successful Sounding Rocket launched by DhumketuX Junior Team.
The DhumketuX Junior Team
Project: Dhumketu Putimach 01 (পুটিমাছ ০১) (small Prototype)
Launch Date and Time: 07 February 2023 at 5:07 PM
Range: 1 KM
Launch degree: 75°
Location: Brahmaputra char Mymensingh (safe & desolate place).
