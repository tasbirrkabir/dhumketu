 Model: SkyBlade Xero Prototype
 SkyBlade Xero is Bangladesh’s first manned drone project, a groundbreaking, open-source, and crowd-funded initiative to develop a single-seater Vertical Take-Off and Landing (VTOL) drone. This pioneering venture, initiated by DhumketuX, seeks to revolutionize aerial mobility while establishing Bangladesh as a prominent hub for aerospace innovation. The project aims to inspire local talent, promote sustainable aviation technology, and engage the community by collaborating with key organizations and clubs.
 General characteristics
 Crew: Single-seater
Length: 3.5 m
Empty Weight: 120+ kg 
Gross Weight: 200 kg
 General Performance
 Maximum Speed: 60 km/h
Cruise Speed: 45 km/h
 Project Goals:
Prototype Completion: Design and develop a functional single-seater VTOL drone prototype that prioritizes safety, efficiency, and sustainability.
Community Collaboration: Engage local universities, aerospace research groups, and tech enthusiasts in the development process.
Open-Source Contribution: Publish design schematics, flight data, and software algorithms to encourage global participation and innovation.
Crowdfunding Campaigns: Involve the public through transparent funding efforts, workshops, and social media outreach.
 Future Prospects:
The success of the SkyBlade Xero prototype will pave the way for scaling up to multi-passenger VTOL designs like the SkyBlade X4. The learnings from this project will contribute to developing urban air mobility solutions and setting the stage for Bangladesh’s leadership in the aerospace industry.
By fostering community engagement and innovation, SkyBlade Xero stands as a symbol of progress and technological ambition for the region.
 Key Collaborators:
1. VTOL Drone Community Bangladesh
2. Bangladesh Aerospace Research Center
3. Metropolitan University Geography and Astronomical Society
4. Andromeda Space & Robotics Research Organizations. [CUET]
5. AirTaxi Bangladesh
