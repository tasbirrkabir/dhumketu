 An Aerospace Research & Development Company of South Asia
 Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Limited (DhumketuX) is a commercial civilian space company, founded by Nahiyan Al Rahman (Oli). The company focuses on small satellite launch vehicles (SSLVs), space-based innovation, and sustainable aerospace technologies. Its mission is to make access to space more affordable, environmentally responsible, and globally competitive—positioning the region at the forefront of the civilian space industry.
 The flagship initiative, the Space Billboard Project , combines advanced technology with sustainability to unlock new possibilities in commercial space utilization across the globe .
 Launch Vehicle
 Ekushey 21
 The Ekushey Rocket is a groundbreaking project by DhumketuX, blending technological innovation with cultural significance. Designed to gather critical atmospheric data, the Ekushey rocket stands as a tribute to the Bangla language movement of 1952.
 View Details 
 Bidrohi SR
 Bidrohi Rocket, developed by Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Limited (DhumketuX), is Bangladesh’s first commercial sub-orbital space rocket, designed to reach sub-orbital space and propel the nation into the global aerospace industry. Named after the iconic poem “Bidrohi” by Kazi Nazrul Islam, the rocket symbolizes the rebellious spirit, resilience, and rising ambition of Bangladesh.
