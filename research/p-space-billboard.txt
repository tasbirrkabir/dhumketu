 Mission Objectives
 The DhumketuX Space Billboard (DSB-1) is a revolutionary solar sail-Type satellite designed to beam messages, logos, and visuals from orbit—visible to vast regions on Earth. It combines orbital advertising with real-time Earth streaming, emergency alerts, and educational content delivery.
 Solar Sail Type Space Billboard V1
 Wide Visibility _
 Displays logos, messages, or emergency signals to millions across continents.
 Live Streaming
 Real-time HD views of Earth and onboard camera feeds broadcasted globally.
 Orbital Advertising
 Run global campaigns with brand visibility from space—seen by millions.
 Space-Based Alerts
 Display international emergency warnings or awareness messages during crises.
 Education & Awareness
 Streams educational content, science missions, and planetary messages to inspire future generations.
