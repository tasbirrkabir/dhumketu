 🤝 Collaboration at DhumketuX
 Collaborate with DhumketuX on Aerospace, UAV & Satellite Systems
 Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Ltd. (DhumketuX) is building Bangladesh’s private ecosystem in aerospace, rocketry, UAVs and satellite systems . We work with universities, industry, agencies and international partners to turn ideas and labs into working hardware, platforms and opportunities .
 📩 Propose a Collaboration 
 📄 View Areas of Collaboration 
 ⚠️ NB: Chemicals, Leadership & Project Control
 We do not sell or supply any aerospace chemicals to any external company or third party. 
 Any project involving aerospace-related chemicals, propellant research or sensitive materials is conducted under DhumketuX lead, infrastructure and conditions .
 Partners may collaborate on research, simulations, design, software and non-sensitive subsystems , while high-risk activities remain within DhumketuX-controlled facilities and protocols.
 This ensures safety, regulatory compliance, quality and responsible use of advanced technologies in Bangladesh.
 Who We Collaborate With
 DhumketuX builds long-term partnerships with institutions and teams that want to create a serious footprint in aerospace, UAVs and satellite systems from Bangladesh.
 🎓 Universities & Institutes
 EEE, CSE, Mechanical, Aerospace, Robotics, Mechatronics, Physics and Applied Science departments building serious tracks in rocketry, UAVs and satellite systems . We co-create labs, projects, internships and innovation cells.
 🏛️ Public Sector & Agencies
 Space-related agencies and public institutions exploring indigenous launch, UAV and satellite capabilities, feasibility studies, trials and concept demonstrations under NDA and clear scopes.
 🏭 Industry & Tech Companies
 Tech startups, SMEs, robotics/IoT/AI/software teams co-developing UAV platforms, aerospace subsystems, satellite-adjacent tools and special-application products.
 🌍 International Partners
 Universities, labs, companies and investors interested in emerging-market aerospace, UAV and satellite-system projects, outreach and ecosystem building in Bangladesh.
 Key Areas of Collaboration
 We are especially interested in projects that touch core aerospace, UAV and satellite-system challenges.
 🚀 Rockets & Propulsion
 Experimental rocket platforms and propulsion test benches
 Flight dynamics, trajectory simulation and structural analysis
 Ground systems, telemetry and mission control software
 Note: Any work touching propellants or regulated materials stays strictly under DhumketuX facilities, leadership and protocols.
 ✈️ UAVs & Autonomy
 Fixed-wing, multirotor and hybrid UAV platforms
 Autopilot integration, sensor fusion and navigation
 Autonomous tracking, swarm ideas and intelligent mission profiles
 🛰️ Space & Satellite Systems
 Small-satellite concept and subsystem studies
 Ground segment tools: mission dashboards, GCS and data visualization
 Earth-observation workflows, SAR/optical data processing and analytics
 University-level CubeSat/small-satellite design projects (concept & ground side)
 🧪 Materials & Test Infrastructure
 High-temperature materials, ablative linings and composites
 Structural testing of aerospace components
 Test stands, load cells, thrust measurement and telemetry setups
 Focus on testing, design and analysis. No aerospace chemical sales are involved.
 💻 Software, Simulation & AI
 Flight simulation, mission planning tools and digital twins
 Ground station and real-time monitoring dashboards
 Computer vision for UAV tracking, navigation and inspection
 Data logging, visualization and analytics for tests and missions
 👩‍🏫 Training & Outreach
 Workshops, bootcamps and hackathons
 Co-branded student challenges and competitions
 Curriculum support for aerospace, UAV and satellite-related courses
 Talent pipelines via internships and training programs
 Collaboration Models
 We keep formats flexible so that universities, organizations and companies can collaborate at a level that matches their vision and resources.
 🤝 MoU & Framework Agreements
 Structured MoUs that define scope, responsibilities, resource sharing, IP, NDA and publication boundaries between DhumketuX and your organization.
 🧪 Joint Projects & R&D Cells
 Co-supervised final-year projects, thesis work, and dedicated R&D cells focusing on rockets, UAVs, satellite systems, materials or mission software.
 🎓 Talent & Internship Pipeline
 Reserved seats for partner institutions in DhumketuX industrial training, internships and trainee engineer tracks; co-designed modules aligned with your curriculum.
 🏭 Productization & Pilots
 Jointly developed prototypes moving towards real products and pilot deployments for survey, monitoring, communication or data services.
 Why Collaborate with DhumketuX?
 🛰️ Real Hardware, Real Labs
 Rockets, UAVs, satellite-related tools, test systems and mission software being developed in the same ecosystem—not just theoretical slideware.
 🇧🇩 Local Roots, Global Mindset
 Focused on Bangladesh, built to global expectations. Ideal for partners who want meaningful impact in South Asia with a technology-first approach.
 🎓 Talent & Skill Development
 Structured internships, industrial training and trainee engineer tracks that channel student and young-engineer talent into real project outcomes.
 🔐 NDA & Professionalism
 We respect confidentiality, IP boundaries and institutional reputation. Sensitive activities are always conducted under strict internal controls.
 🧭 Long-Term Ecosystem View
 We aim to build a sustainable aerospace and space-tech ecosystem, not one-off publicity projects. That shapes how we choose and run collaborations.
 How a Collaboration Typically Starts
 Initial Contact: You email us or invite us for a meeting with a short description of your organization and collaboration idea.
 Exploratory Discussion: Online or in-person discussions to understand goals, constraints, and what you want your students / engineers / teams to achieve.
 Scope & Model Drafting: Decide whether it’s an MoU, project, pilot or training partnership. Align on IP, NDA and risk boundaries.
 Agreement & Kick-off: Finalize terms, optionally announce the collaboration, and start with a clear roadmap, milestones and responsibilities.
