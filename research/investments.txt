 Ecosystem
 Parent Ecosystem
 Antimatter Industries
 Antimatter Industries is the wider multinational advanced-technology,
 engineering and industrial ecosystem associated with DhumketuX and its
 portfolio of companies, strategic platforms and mission-driven organizations.
 Multinational Advanced Technology & Engineering Ecosystem
 One ecosystem. Multiple industries. Shared ambition.
 Antimatter Industries connects companies working across aerospace, UAVs,
 propulsion, artificial intelligence, robotics, semiconductors, maritime
 technology, energy, lubricants, tourism, media, consumer products, strategic
 research and venture creation.
 DhumketuX remains the principal aerospace and space-technology company
 within this ecosystem. Each company maintains a defined operational focus,
 governance model and investment structure while benefiting from strategic
 coordination across the group.
 Parent ecosystem: Antimatter Industries
 Aerospace core: Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Limited
 Operating model: independent companies with shared strategic capability
 Website: AntimatterIndustries.net
 Important Investment Policy
 Mission stability comes before short-term capital.
 DhumketuX does not generally offer direct equity in the main DhumketuX company to local external investors.
 The preferred models are project-based investment, strategic partnership, joint venture and investment through DhumketuX sister companies.
 Direct investment in the core DhumketuX company may be considered in special cases for qualified strategic foreign or institutional investors.
 Any core-equity discussion is subject to valuation, strategic alignment, governance, compliance and long-term commitment.
 Short-term speculative investment is not considered suitable for DhumketuX.
 Sensitive, regulated or dual-use programs may require government approval and may not be open to general external investment.
 Antimatter Industries companies may maintain separate investment policies according to their sectors.

 6. Contribution-Based Ownership
 Idea and Intellectual-Property Models
 Commercial rights are structured according to who contributes the
 concept, technology, capital, execution, market access and long-term operation.
 Idea from DhumketuX
 DhumketuX may contribute the concept, research, architecture, prototype, IP and engineering team.
 The partner may contribute capital, manufacturing, distribution, market access or international capability.
 Idea from Investor or Partner
 The partner may contribute the business concept, customer access, distribution network, existing technology or commercial strategy.
 DhumketuX may contribute engineering, product development, testing, integration and program execution.
 Jointly Developed Idea
 The agreement may establish joint IP, field-specific rights, geographic rights, licensing, royalties, revenue sharing or commercialization rights.

 12. From Inquiry to Agreement
 Investment and Partnership Process
 A structured process helps both sides assess strategic fit,
 technical feasibility, compliance and commercial value.
 01
 Initial Inquiry
 Submit organization, country, sector, proposed scale, preferred company and partnership model.
 02
 Internal Review
 Evaluate strategic alignment, feasibility, commercial potential, regulation and required resources.
 03
 Introductory Meeting
 Qualified parties discuss objectives, contribution, timeline and potential structure.
 04
 Confidentiality
 Sign an NDA where sensitive information must be exchanged.
 05
 Concept Note
 Define scope, budget, responsibilities, milestones, ownership and expected outcomes.
 06
 Due Diligence
 Conduct legal, financial, technical, compliance and commercial review.
 07
 Final Agreement
 Formalize the relationship through the appropriate project, investment, partnership or JV agreement.
