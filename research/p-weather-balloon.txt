 Mission Objectives
 The HAWB (High-Altitude Weather Balloon) Project by DhumketuX aims to explore and understand the dynamics of Earth’s upper atmosphere, specifically the stratosphere, to gather vital data on weather patterns, climate change, and atmospheric conditions. This mission utilizes high-altitude weather balloons equipped with advanced sensors and instruments to collect real-time data and images, contributing to global environmental research, scientific studies, and technological advancements.
 HAWB (High-Altitude Weather Balloon)
 Monitor Conditions
 Gather data on temperature, pressure, humidity, and radiation levels at high altitudes, forecasting and climate modeling.
 Test Advanced Technologies
 Leverage cutting-edge sensor systems and advanced data transmission technologies for future space exploration and satellite development.
 Foster Educational
 Provide students and researchers with hands-on experience in space science and atmospheric research, promoting STEM education and innovation.
 Contribute to Global 
 Support global initiatives on climate change, environmental monitoring, and atmospheric science through open-source data sharing.
