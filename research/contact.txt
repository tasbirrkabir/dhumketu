 To help us respond faster, please use a clear subject line and include all relevant details about your request.

 How to Structure Your Message
 To help us respond effectively, please include the following details in your email or contact form submission:
 Full Name
 Email Address and optional mobile / WhatsApp
 Organization / University / Company
 Country & City
 Area of Interest (Collaboration, Internship, Media, Vendor, etc.)
 Subject (one clear line summarizing your request)
 Message / Concept Summary (2–3 short paragraphs)
 If you are proposing a technical collaboration, you can also add your team’s background, previous work links (GitHub, publications, demos) and any initial concept notes as PDF.

 Location & Lab Visits
 DhumketuX operates from Bangladesh with R&D, testing and collaboration activities running with local and international partners. For security and safety reasons, visits are by appointment only.
 Clearly mention your purpose of visit
 Include number of visitors and preferred dates
 Wait for written confirmation and instructions
 To request a visit, email xdhumketu@gmail.com with the subject: “Visit Request – [Your Organization]”.
 Frequently Asked Questions
 Can students or university clubs contact DhumketuX directly?
 Yes. We encourage serious emails from university teams, clubs and research groups working on rocketry, UAVs, satellites or related technologies. Introduce your team, show what you are already doing and explain what type of collaboration you are looking for.
 Do you reply to every email?
 We try to respond to all relevant, clearly written messages. However, due to workload and project schedules, some inquiries may take longer or may not move forward. Clear subjects and focused content make it easier to respond.
 Can I visit the DhumketuX lab or office?
 Visits are by appointment only and depend on security, active testing schedules and NDA requirements. Please request a visit in advance by email with a clear purpose and proposed dates.
 Can international partners work with DhumketuX?
 Yes. We welcome international collaboration in joint R&D, pilot projects, technology co-development and training programs. Use the subject “International Collaboration – [Your Organization]” when contacting us.
 Can I sponsor or support student projects through DhumketuX?
 Yes, we are open to curated student programs, challenges and sponsorships focused on aerospace, UAVs, rocketry, electronics and advanced engineering. Share your idea and scope via email and we will explore possibilities.
 Still not sure which email to use or how to frame your message? Send a brief introduction to xdhumketu@gmail.com and we’ll route it to the right person inside DhumketuX.
