 Ibn Khaldun Engine
 Ibn Khaldun | Ibn Khaldun Vacuum (RVac)
 Named after the great scholar Ibn Khaldun, this 35 kN class engine is designed to power Small Satellite Launch Vehicles (SSLVs) and scale up to cluster engine configurations. With high-thrust performance and reliability, it supports both commercial and scientific space missions.
