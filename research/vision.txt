 Vision · Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Ltd.
 Building Space Infrastructure from Bangladesh & Asia for the Global Market
 Our long-term vision at DhumketuX is clear: build space billboard, Earth observation and space-based internet constellations from Bangladesh and Asia that serve global customers. We want to connect people, data and brands through responsible, sustainable space infrastructure.
 From orbital media platforms to Earth observation satellites and LEO connectivity, DhumketuX aims to give our region a serious role in the global space economy.
 Our Three Core Space Pillars
 DhumketuX’s long-term vision is structured around three interconnected pillars that together form an integrated space infrastructure strategy.
 📡
 Space Billboard & Orbital Media
 We envision a formation of small satellites acting as “pixels” in orbit—creating temporary patterns and messages in the sky. Our goal is to build a responsible space billboard platform for global and Asian brands, with strict control over visibility, brightness and orbital safety.
 High-precision formation flying
 Scheduled visibility windows
 Compliance with international space guidelines
 🌍
 Earth Observation Constellations
 We plan to operate small satellite constellations providing frequent coverage over key Asian geographies, coastal regions and global economic corridors—delivering not just pictures, but analytics and insights-as-a-service for agriculture, climate, urban planning and disaster resilience.
 Crop & food system monitoring
 Coastal & river change detection
 Smart city & infrastructure analytics
 📶
 Space-Based Internet Service
 We aim to develop regional LEO connectivity constellations that extend high-speed internet to remote, coastal and disaster-prone areas—especially across Asia—while supporting maritime and aviation connectivity.
 Backup connectivity during outages
 Links for remote schools & clinics
 Hybrid with fiber, 5G & community Wi-Fi
 Special Focus · Bangladesh
 Space Vision Originating from Bangladesh
 While our markets are global and Asian, our engineering and mission design roots are firmly in Bangladesh . DhumketuX wants to build real satellite hardware, software and operations from here—creating high-skilled jobs in aerospace, electronics, software, AI and data science.
 Better flood, river and cyclone monitoring via EO constellations
 Space-based internet as backup and extension for char areas, coastal zones and remote communities
 New economic opportunities through orbital media, data services and local partnerships

 A Unified Space Infrastructure Strategy
 Each pillar is powerful on its own, but the true impact appears when they work together:
 Space Billboard & Orbital Media – attracts global attention and revenue while demonstrating advanced formation-flying and control capabilities.
 Earth Observation Constellations – supply high-value data and analytics for governments, enterprises and research communities.
 Space-Based Internet – delivers those services and insights anywhere, including remote and mobile users.
 Orbital media → attention & revenue → EO data & analytics → satellite internet delivery → more users & developers → stronger ecosystem in Bangladesh, Asia and globally.
 Responsible & Sustainable Space
 Doing Space the Right Way
 Our vision only makes sense if it is safe, ethical and sustainable. DhumketuX is committed to:
 Protecting the night sky – designing orbital media with controlled brightness and limited visibility windows.
 Minimizing orbital debris – following debris-mitigation standards and implementing clear end-of-life and de-orbit strategies.
 Ethical data & connectivity – respecting privacy and regulations, supporting fair access for education and public-good projects, and being transparent about how our data and services are used.
 Partnerships, Talent & Open Innovation
 To make this vision real, DhumketuX partners with universities, research labs, startups and industry players across Bangladesh, Asia and the wider world. We are building a long-term platform where engineers, scientists, designers and entrepreneurs can work on real satellite missions.
 Satellite systems & mission design
 Orbital mechanics & formation flying
 EO data science, AI & analytics
 RF communications, antennas & ground segment
 Onboard & mission software development
 Vision in One Line
 DhumketuX aims to build space billboard, Earth observation and space-based internet constellations from Bangladesh and Asia for the global market—combining commercial innovation with responsible, sustainable use of orbit.

 Bangladesh Space Center (BSC)
 Bangladesh is taking its first bold steps into the future of space exploration.  
  Proposed by Nahiyan Al Rahman, Founder & CEO of Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Limited (DhumketuX), the Bangladesh Space Center will be a national hub for aerospace research, education, and innovation. 
  This visionary project is designed with two core sectors:  
 1️⃣ Student & General Sector – Inspiring young minds, building a patriotic, skilled generation ready for space and science.  
 2️⃣ Academic & Research Sector – Bringing together universities, scientists, and engineers to push the boundaries of aerospace technology in Bangladesh.  
 From rocketry and satellites to defense innovation and global collaboration, this initiative aims to position Bangladesh as a rising space power.
