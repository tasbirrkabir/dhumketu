🚀 Careers · DhumketuX
		
		
		
Careers at DhumketuX
		
		
		
		Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Ltd. (DhumketuX) is building Bangladesh’s private ecosystem in 
rocketry, UAVs, satellite-related systems and mission software
. If you want to work on real hardware, real tests and real missions—not just slides and theory—this is where you belong.
		
		
		
		&gt; We are looking for people who want to 
build, learn fast and take responsibility
 in a frontier aerospace startup.
		
		
		
			
			
📩 Apply Now – xdhumketu@gmail.com
			
			
			
📚 Explore Career Paths
			
		
		
	
	
	
	
	
		
		
Why Work at DhumketuX?
		
		
		
		Joining DhumketuX means stepping into an early-stage aerospace ecosystem where your work has direct impact on 
rockets, UAV platforms, satellite-related tools and mission systems
 being built in Bangladesh.
		
		
		
			
			
				
				
🔧 Real Engineering
				
				
				
				Design, build and test rockets, UAVs, ground systems, avionics and test setups. See your work move from concept to hardware and flight.
				
			
			
			
			
				
				
🚀 Build a New Industry
				
				
				
				Help establish long-term aerospace, UAV and satellite capabilities inside Bangladesh instead of only consuming technology from abroad.
				
			
			
			
			
				
				
📚 Fast Learning & Ownership
				
				
				
				Small, focused teams mean you see end-to-end lifecycles and own modules, documentation and results—not just perform small tasks.
				
			
			
		
		
	
	
	
	
	
		
		
Our Work Culture
		
		
		
		DhumketuX is still a growing company. That means our culture is intense, practical and focused on outcomes. We expect every team member to contribute actively, learn continuously and respect safety and confidentiality boundaries.
		
		
		
			
Hands-on work:
 you will see and touch hardware, visit test sites and work directly with tools and code (as relevant).
			
Documentation-first mindset:
 schematics, test logs, notebooks, version control and clear write-ups matter as much as the build.
			
Safety-aware:
 high-risk activities follow strict procedures and leadership oversight; you must respect limits and checklists.
			
NDA and confidentiality:
 many projects are sensitive. Respecting internal boundaries is non-negotiable.
			
Merit & responsibility:
 we focus on contribution, reliability and ownership—not just job titles.
		
		
	
	
	
	
	
		
		
Role Categories at DhumketuX
		
		
		
			
			
			
				
				
🛠️ Engineering & R&D
				
				
				
					
Rocketry & Propulsion:
 experimental rockets, static & dynamic thrust tests, structural and thermal design.
					
UAV & Flight Systems:
 fixed-wing, VTOL and multirotor design, integration and performance tuning.
					
Avionics & Embedded Systems:
 PCB design, firmware, sensor fusion, RF and telemetry links.
					
Software & Mission Systems:
 GCS software, dashboards, simulations, mission planning and data pipelines.
					
Materials & Test:
 composite and metallic structures, test rigs, validation and characterization.
				
				
			
			
			
			
			
				
				
📊 Business, Operations & Support
				
				
				
					
Project & Program Coordination:
 roadmaps, timelines, meeting notes and follow-ups.
					
Supply Chain & Vendors:
 sourcing components, managing fabrication and logistics.
					
Operations & Admin:
 lab and office operations, HR basics, test and event support.
					
Content & Communication:
 technical documentation, presentations and curated outreach (within NDA limits).
				
				
			
			
			
			
			
				
				
🎓 Students & Early-Career
				
				
				
					
Industrial Training:
 short, structured exposure to lab culture and aerospace fundamentals.
					
Internships:
 3–6 month roles with real tasks, mentor support and evaluation.
					
Trainee Engineer Tracks:
 longer, intensive paths designed to grow into core roles.
				
				
				
				
				Certificates and experience letters are issued only upon full completion of the agreed timeline, terms and NDA conditions.
				
			
			
		
		
	
	
	
	
	
		
		
Company Membership – Joining the Core DhumketuX Team
		
		
		
		Beyond short-term training or internships, DhumketuX uses the concept of 
Company Membership
 for individuals who join the core team with deeper access, responsibility and long-term involvement. Company Membership is offered only to those who successfully pass our selection process.
		
		
		
			
			
			
				
				
✅ Company Membership Requirements
				
				
				
					
Minimum experience:
 at least 
1–2 years of relevant industry experience
.
					
Selection process:
 aptitude and/or technical tests, interviews and practical assessments.
					
Formal employment:
 full-time roles typically require 
3+ years of industry experience
 plus a strong record of hands-on delivery.
				
				
			
			
			
			
			
				
				
🎯 Company Members Enjoy
				
				
				
					
More extensive lab access for advanced projects (within safety and NDA limits).
					
Involvement in critical, cutting-edge initiatives in rockets, UAVs and satellite-related systems.
					
Clear responsibilities and recognition within the DhumketuX team structure.
					
Pathways towards comprehensive employment benefits as DhumketuX scales.
				
				
				
				
				Exact benefits and terms may vary by role, project and timing; details are discussed individually during the offer stage.
				
			
			
		
		
	
	
	
	
	
		
		
Internship Requirements & Community Pathways
		
		
		
		Internship and trainee roles are designed for serious learners who are already putting in effort through projects or community work. We prefer candidates who have shown commitment, not just curiosity.
		
		
		
			
			
			
				
				
📌 Internship Requirements
				
				
				
					
Experience:
 ideally 
6 months to 1 year
 of relevant experience (projects, labs or prior work).
					
Project exposure:
 academic projects, personal builds or open-source contributions linked to aerospace, UAVs, robotics, embedded systems or software.
					
Focused interest:
 clear motivation to work in rockets, UAVs, embedded systems, mission software or related areas.
				
				
				
				
				We prioritize candidates who can show completed work (even small projects) with basic documentation over those with only theoretical knowledge.
				
			
			
			
			
			
				
				
🌐 Community Pathways
				
				
				
				Experience or active membership in serious technical communities strongly boosts your chances, such as:
				
				
				
					
High Powered Rocketry Community Bangladesh (HPRCB)
					
Advanced Robotics “ROS & ROS2” Community Bangladesh
					
FPGA & VLSI Community Bangladesh
					
VTOL Drone Bangladesh
					
Other serious engineering communities with project-based work
				
				
				
				
				Active participation shows that you are serious, persistent and already exposed to real engineering problems—not just textbooks.
				
			
			
		
		
	
	
	
	
	
		
		
How to Prepare Through Community & Self-Learning
		
		
		
		You do not need to wait for a formal posting to start preparing for DhumketuX. You can build your profile through community, self-learning and consistent project work.
		
		
		
			
Register & Join Communities:
 connect with serious engineering communities (HPRCB, ROS/ROS2, FPGA & VLSI, VTOL Drone Bangladesh, etc.) as they often collaborate with industry and advanced projects.
			
Engage & Contribute:
 take part in discussions, share short write-ups, help organize small initiatives or podcasts, and work on community projects.
			
Prepare & Document:
 use community resources and your own practice to build projects, then document them clearly so they can be evaluated during DhumketuX assessments.
		
		
		
		
		This route does not guarantee selection—but it significantly strengthens your profile and readiness for DhumketuX or any serious aerospace role.
		
	
	
	
	
	
		
		
What We Look For in Candidates
		
		
		
			
			
			
				
				
🧑‍🎓 Interns & Junior Roles
				
				
				
					
Solid fundamentals in your discipline (EEE, CSE, Mechanical, Aerospace, Robotics, etc.).
					
Proof of effort: academic projects, hobby builds, community work or simple prototypes.
					
Good written and verbal communication, willingness to ask questions and accept feedback.
					
Discipline in following safety, NDA and process instructions.
				
				
			
			
			
			
			
				
				
🧱 Company Members & Mid-Level
				
				
				
					
At least 
1–2 years of industrial experience
 in a relevant field.
					
Clear examples of owning modules, tasks or subsystems from start to completion.
					
Ability to work with partial specifications, constraints and evolving requirements.
					
Respect for safety, confidentiality, documentation and team workflows.
				
				
			
			
			
			
			
				
				
🧑‍🏫 Senior & Supervisory Roles
				
				
				
					
Typically 
5+ years
 (professionals) or 
12+ years
 (supervisors) of relevant experience.
					
Ability to architect systems, review designs and manage technical and operational risk.
					
Experience leading teams or mentoring engineers in demanding environments.
					
Strong communication and decision-making under constraints and uncertainty.
