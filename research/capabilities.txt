 Building Indigenous Aerospace Technology from Bangladesh
 Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Ltd. (DhumketuX) develops integrated systems across rocketry, UAVs, satellite-related systems, avionics and mission software . Our goal is simple: to design, assemble, test and sustain high-impact aerospace technologies from within Bangladesh.

 Overview of Our Technological Capabilities
 DhumketuX works at the intersection of air, space and ground systems , focusing on full-stack engineering rather than isolated parts. From flight platforms and propulsion to avionics, telemetry, software and testing, we design systems that can evolve and scale inside Bangladesh.
 🚀 Rocket Systems & Testbeds
 Concept-to-test development of small and medium rocket systems, including ground propulsion test infrastructure and flight readiness workflows.
 ✈️ UAV Platforms & Autonomy
 Design and integration of fixed-wing and multirotor UAV systems, propulsion, power, autonomy and mission-focused payloads.
 🛰️ Satellite & Space-Related Systems
 Ground segment concepts, mission modelling and data systems for future satellite and space-related projects.
 📡 Avionics, Telemetry & Ground Control
 Onboard electronics, embedded systems, communication links and ground control software for full-stack mission awareness.
 1. Integrated Aerospace & Flight Systems
 We treat every project as a complete system, not just a collection of parts. Our engineering approach combines mechanical structures, electronics, software, telemetry and operations into a single mission-driven framework.
 Concept-to-test lifecycle for rockets, UAV platforms and high-altitude systems.
 Alignment of mechanical, electrical, software and field operations.
 Platform, payload, ground segment and data analysis under one roof.
 2. Rocket Systems & Propulsion Testbeds
 DhumketuX develops small and medium-scale rocket systems as testbeds for future launch and experimental platforms, supported by dedicated propulsion testing infrastructure.
 📐 Rocket Design & Configuration
 Structural layout and mass budgeting.
 Fin, airframe and stability optimization.
 Modular segments for different payloads and mission profiles.
 🧪 Propulsion Test Infrastructure
 Ground test stands with integrated load cells for thrust measurement.
 Pressure and temperature data acquisition for performance analysis.
 Real-time test visualization and structured test campaign planning.
 🚀 Flight Readiness Workflow
 Pre-flight checklists and validation procedures.
 Flight data logging and post-flight analysis.
 Incremental test levels for safe performance growth.
 All propulsion and rocket-related activities are conducted under strict safety, regulatory and internal procedural frameworks.
 3. UAV Platforms & Autonomous Systems
 DhumketuX designs and integrates both fixed-wing and multirotor UAV platforms for real-world missions such as surveillance, mapping and experimental payload testing.
 ✈️ Airframe Design & Integration
 Layout optimization for endurance, range and payload.
 Structural integration of propulsion, avionics and payload modules.
 Balance between manufacturability and performance.
 🔋 Propulsion & Power Integration
 Motor and propeller selection and performance estimation.
 ESC, battery and power distribution design.
 Thermal and current safety margins for long missions.
 🤖 Autonomous Flight & Payloads
 Autopilot integration and waypoint missions.
 Manual override, return-to-home and basic failsafe behavior.
 EO/IR cameras, mapping payloads and experimental sensor modules.
 4. Satellite & Space-Related Systems
 DhumketuX is gradually building foundational capabilities for future satellite and space-related programs, focusing on ground segment, mission modelling and experimental payload concepts.
 📡 Ground Segment & Data
 Conceptual ground station architectures.
 Tracking, telemetry and control concepts for small missions.
 Mission monitoring and data visualization interfaces.
 🛰️ Small Payload Concepts
 Preliminary design studies for experimental payloads.
 Academic collaboration for space-related research directions.
 Focus on realistic, incremental capability-building.
 🧮 Mission Simulation & Modelling
 Orbit and coverage simulations.
 Communication window analysis.
 Scenario modelling for future satellite concepts.
 5. Avionics, Guidance & Embedded Systems
 Avionics is the nervous system of our rockets and UAVs. DhumketuX designs flight computers, embedded firmware and sensor integration stacks tailored to mission needs.
 🧠 Flight Computers & Sensors
 Microcontroller and embedded-compute-based architectures.
 Integration of IMU, GNSS, barometer, magnetometer and other sensors.
 Power conditioning and protection for avionics stacks.
 ⚙️ Embedded Firmware & Control
 Sensor fusion and flight data computation.
 Actuator control (servos, motor controllers, valves, relays).
 Health monitoring and fault detection logic.
 📍 Guidance & Navigation Basics
 Attitude and trajectory estimation.
 Integration with mission planning tools.
 Hardware-in-the-loop tests and bench simulations.
 6. Telemetry, Communications & Ground Control
 Reliable communication between ground and airborne or rocket systems is essential. DhumketuX designs end-to-end telemetry and ground control solutions.
 📡 Telemetry Links
 RF-based communication setups for live position, speed, altitude and health data.
 Configurable data rates and packet structures.
 Robust logging for replay and diagnostics.
 🖥️ Ground Control Stations
 Custom dashboards for real-time monitoring.
 Graphs, gauges and map-based mission views.
 Multi-channel logging for long-duration tests.
 📊 Data Logging & Analysis
 Structured log storage for each test and mission.
 Mission replay and performance insight extraction.
 Data-driven design iteration and optimization.
 7. Simulation, Modelling & Software Tools
 Before hardware is built or launched, DhumketuX relies on simulation and modelling to reduce risk and concentrate testing where it matters most.
 Flight & Trajectory Simulation: performance modelling for rocket and UAV missions, exploring sensitivities in mass, drag, thrust and control parameters.
 Structural & System-Level Studies: conceptual evaluations of loads and margins to highlight critical regions early.
 Custom Tools & Dashboards: internal web-based tools for test visualization, configuration management and reporting.
 8. Materials, Structures & Manufacturing
 We design structures and choose materials based on performance, safety and the realities of Bangladesh’s manufacturing ecosystem.
 🏗️ Structural Design
 Rocket bodies, UAV wings and support structures.
 Load path analysis and reinforcement strategies.
 Balance of stiffness, mass and manufacturability.
 🔩 Materials & Processes
 Use of metals, composites and hybrid structures.
 Adaptation to locally available materials and tooling.
 Design-for-manufacture strategies with local workshops.
 9. Test, Validation & Quality Workflow
 Testing is central to DhumketuX engineering culture. Every lab or field test is treated as a structured learning opportunity.
 Ground & Static Tests: thrust stand runs, structural fit checks, electronics verification and sensor calibration.
 Field Trials & Flight Tests: incremental test levels, safety planning, go/no-go criteria and structured debriefs.
 Documentation & Traceability: test reports, configuration tracking, change logs and corrective action workflows.
