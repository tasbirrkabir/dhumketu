 Social Responsibilities at DhumketuX
 At Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Ltd. (DhumketuX), rockets, UAVs and satellite-related systems are only one side of our mission. The other side is social: using aerospace and advanced technology to inspire, educate and empower people across Bangladesh in a safe, ethical and inclusive way.
 📩 Contact Outreach Team
 📚 View Our Pillars
 Our Social Responsibility Framework
 We believe every high-tech company in Bangladesh must also act as a teacher, guide and community partner. DhumketuX structures its social responsibilities around four core pillars:
 🛰️ STEM & Aerospace Education
 Making space, rocketry, UAVs and satellite systems understandable and exciting for students and the general public.
 👩‍🎓 Youth Development & Skills
 Transforming curiosity into capability through structured training, internships and real project responsibilities.
 ⚖️ Ethical & Responsible Innovation
 Putting safety, law, NDA and ethical use of technology ahead of shortcuts or short-term gains.
 🤝 Community & Inclusion
 Engaging schools, campuses and communities across Bangladesh—not only a few major institutions.
 1. STEM & Aerospace Education
 For many students in Bangladesh, aerospace feels distant and “for other countries only.” DhumketuX works to make space, flight and high-tech engineering visible and understandable inside our own classrooms and campuses.
 🎓 Talks & Campus Seminars
 Sessions on rocketry, UAVs, satellite systems, telemetry and mission control—designed for EEE, CSE, Mechanical, Aerospace and related departments.
 🧪 Workshops & Demonstrations
 Model rockets, gliders, UAV basics and simple telemetry demonstrations that turn theory into hands-on experiences for students.
 📚 Learning Roadmaps
 Guidance on open-source courses and resources (e.g., SAR basics, avionics, embedded systems, flight control) so students can continue learning independently.
 2. Youth Development & Skill-Building
 Bangladesh has a massive pool of talented young people. Our responsibility is to give them pathways to grow from **interested observers** into **capable contributors** in aerospace and advanced technology.
 📘 Structured Programs
 Industrial training, internships and trainee engineer tracks that convert classroom theory into real-world work on rockets, UAVs, test systems and mission software.
 🧩 Real Responsibilities
 Students and young engineers work on meaningful modules—CAD design, electronics, embedded code, simulations, documentation, testing support and more.
 🧭 Mentorship & Feedback
 Regular guidance from experienced engineers and leads on debugging, documentation, problem-solving and long-term career planning.
 3. Ethical & Responsible Innovation
 High-impact technologies must be developed with responsibility. For DhumketuX, ethical and safe innovation is a core social duty, not just an internal policy.
 🔐 NDA & Confidentiality Culture: serious contributors and collaborators work under clear Non-Disclosure Agreements, learning how professional engineering teams operate.
 ⚠️ Safety & Compliance First: high-risk operations and testing are done under controlled conditions, with documented procedures and leadership oversight.
 🚫 No Aerospace Chemical Sales to Third Parties: DhumketuX does not sell or supply aerospace chemicals to any external company or third party. Projects involving such materials are performed only under DhumketuX lead, infrastructure and conditions.
 📜 Documentation & Traceability: emphasis on logs, reports and design history so decisions can be reviewed and improved over time.
 4. Community Engagement & Inclusion
 We don’t want aerospace to stay limited to a few people or campuses. DhumketuX aims to help build a culture around technology, not just a company.
 🏫 Campus & Club Support
 Engagement with student clubs, tech societies and robotics groups to guide serious, long-term aerospace and UAV projects.
 🌐 Beyond Big Cities
 Whenever possible, we seek ways to reach students and enthusiasts outside the main city centers, not just major universities.
 Working with Schools, Colleges & Universities
 DhumketuX collaborates with educational institutions that want to build real, long-term capacity in aerospace, UAVs and satellite systems.
 Co-designing seminars, guest lectures and aerospace awareness programs.
 Hosting workshops, bootcamps and project clinics aligned with your curriculum.
 Exploring MoUs and structured collaboration models for long-term engagement.
 Supporting student project themes that connect to real engineering needs.
 Impact & Future Goals
 Over time, DhumketuX aims to track and grow its social impact in measurable ways, such as:
 Number of students and institutions engaged through training, talks and programs.
 Number of interns and trainee engineers progressing into key technical roles.
 Growth of local labs, teams and startups working in aerospace, UAVs and satellite-related areas.
 High-level success stories (shared without breaking NDAs) that showcase how STEM and aerospace programs change lives.
 Our long-term vision is a Bangladesh where a student who loves space, aviation or robotics can find meaningful, high-tech work without having to leave the country.
 How You Can Get Involved
 🧑‍🎓 For Students
 Follow DhumketuX official channels for announcements about training, internships and workshops.
 Build small but serious projects, document them and use them when you apply for programs.
 Join seminars and Q&A sessions to understand real-world engineering workflows.
 🏫 For Teachers & Academic Leaders
 Invite DhumketuX for seminars, panel talks or exploratory meetings.
 Plan joint activities that fit your department’s curriculum and level.
 Encourage students to focus on long-term, well-documented projects rather than just quick demos.
 🤝 For Organizations & Supporters
 Explore co-branded STEM and aerospace education initiatives.
 Support long-term programs focused on youth development and skill-building.
 Partner with DhumketuX to design impactful projects for schools and universities.
