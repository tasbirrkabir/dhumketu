 Mission Objectives
 Micro Satellite ‘Bijoy’ is a groundbreaking satellite project by DhumketuX, designed to advance Bangladesh’s space capabilities. Set for launch in 2027, Bijoy will focus on Earth observation, climate monitoring, and communication technology testing.
 Bijoy Micro Satellite
 Adv. Sensors
 To measure temperature, pressure, humidity, and other key atmospheric conditions for space research and environmental monitoring.
 Com. Systems
 Testing high-efficiency data transmission systems to improve communication for space-based platforms and enhance satellite data transfer capabilities.
 Compact Design
 Integrated solar panels for improved energy efficiency and continuous real-time communication with ground stations for data transmission.
 Real-Time Data
 Continuous com. with Earth for real-time data collection and mission updates throughout the satellite’s operational lifespan.
