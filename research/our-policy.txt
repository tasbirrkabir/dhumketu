 📜 Our Policy & Core Values · DhumketuX
 Engineering with Integrity, Safety and Responsibility
 Dhumketu Exploration Technologies Ltd. (DhumketuX) is building long-term capability in rocketry, UAVs, satellite-related systems, avionics and mission software from Bangladesh. Our work is guided by clear policies and core values that prioritize safety, ethics, responsible technology use and national capacity building.
 This page outlines how we think about safety, confidentiality, collaboration, student engagement and the responsible use of high-impact technologies.
 1. Our Core Values
 Our core values are not slogans—they are filters for everyday decisions at DhumketuX, from small design choices to long-term partnerships.
 🔎 Integrity & Transparency
 Honest communication, realistic claims and traceable decisions. We avoid exaggeration and let data, documentation and results speak for themselves.
 ⚠️ Safety First
 No test, schedule or publicity is more important than safety. Every team member has the right to stop a test if something looks or feels unsafe.
 🛠️ Engineering Excellence & Discipline
 We focus on systems that work reliably in the real world: strong fundamentals, iterative improvement and a documentation-first mindset.
 📚 Learning, Curiosity & Humility
 We learn from experiments, tests and mistakes. Questions are welcome, feedback is valued and better ideas can come from anyone on the team.
 🇧🇩 National Capacity Building
 We see DhumketuX as part of a wider ecosystem: building skills, tools and know-how inside Bangladesh through projects, labs and structured programs.
 🤝 Respect & Collaboration
 We work in multidisciplinary teams and treat each other with respect—no ego, no discrimination, just shared focus on building high-quality systems.
 🌍 Responsibility & Sustainability
 We think long-term about the impact of our technologies, minimizing unnecessary risk and waste while designing systems that can be maintained and upgraded.
 2. Ethics, Compliance & Governance
 Because we work with high-impact technologies, DhumketuX follows strict ethics and compliance standards covering law, NDA, intellectual property and fair practice.
 ⚖️ Legal & Regulatory Compliance
 Respect for all applicable laws and regulations of Bangladesh.
 Working with relevant authorities and institutions where needed.
 Updating internal practices as policies and rules evolve.
 🔐 NDA, Confidentiality & IP
 Many projects require signed Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA).
 Internal designs, test data, code and documents are not shared externally without written approval.
 Intellectual property (IP) ownership is defined clearly in contracts, MoUs or project agreements.
 🧭 Anti-Corruption & Fair Practice
 Zero tolerance for unethical shortcuts or hidden deals.
 Structured processes for collaborations and procurement.
 Selection based on merit, work ethic and fit—not politics or favoritism.
 3. Safety & Risk Management Policy
 With rocketry, high-energy tests, UAVs and electronics, safety is not optional. It is built into how we plan, test and operate.
 🛡️ Safety Principles
 No experiment is worth risking life or long-term health.
 Any team member can call a stop if they see something unsafe.
 Safety is prioritized over timelines or publicity.
 🏭 Labs, Workshops & Test Sites
 Written procedures and checklists for critical operations.
 Mandatory use of appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
 Controlled access to high-risk areas during sensitive tests.
 📘 Documentation & Incident Learning
 Logging of tests, anomalies, near-misses and incidents.
 Root cause analysis and corrective measures when issues arise.
 Continuous updating of procedures based on lessons learned.
 4. Research, Learning & Student Engagement Policy
 DhumketuX actively works with students, interns and young engineers through structured programs that focus on learning, contribution and real-world exposure.
 4.1 Fair Access & Clear Expectations
 Transparent communication about program structure, duration and workload.
 Focus on meaningful tasks, not just observation or symbolic work.
 Clear understanding of how each participant’s work fits into larger projects.
 4.2 Certificates & Experience Records
 To protect the value of DhumketuX certifications, we maintain strict conditions on completion and verification.
 Certificates (industrial training, internship, trainee engineer and other tracks) are issued only when:
 The candidate has completed the full agreed timeline.
 Attendance, participation and basic documentation are verified.
 Minimum assigned tasks and contributions are completed.
 If someone does not complete the terms, conditions or time period, their internship or training may be automatically cancelled and no certificate will be issued.
 All participants must fully respect NDA obligations and confidentiality rules.
 4.3 Academic Integrity
 No tolerance for plagiarism in reports, code, designs or presentations.
 Participants are encouraged to use proper citations and honest reporting of results.
 We value original thinking, careful validation and clear explanation over superficial speed.
 5. Responsible Use of Technology
 Rocketry, UAVs and satellite-related systems can create enormous value—but they must be used carefully and responsibly. DhumketuX is committed to positive and compliant applications.
 Focusing on applications that support research, connectivity, science, education and long-term national capability.
 Avoiding reckless or unregulated deployment of advanced technologies.
 Ensuring that any external use of DhumketuX technology is properly documented, approved and aligned with our policies.
 6. Materials & Aerospace Chemicals Policy
 Handling aerospace-related materials and chemicals requires strict safety, compliance and leadership oversight.
 NB: DhumketuX does not sell or provide any aerospace chemicals to external companies, teams or individuals.
 All aerospace-related chemicals and sensitive materials are managed internally under controlled conditions.
 Most projects are executed under DhumketuX leadership and our conditions, with strict procedures for safety and compliance.
 We do not support informal, unregulated or unsafe use of advanced materials or processes.
 7. Collaboration, Partnerships & Transparency
 DhumketuX works with universities, research groups and organizations under clear written understandings that protect safety, confidentiality and intellectual property.
 Use of MoUs or project agreements that define scope, responsibilities and boundaries.
 Transparent communication of goals, limitations and realistic timelines.
 Co-designing programs, labs or training modules that align with our core capabilities.
 8. Decision-Making Framework
 Every major decision at DhumketuX passes through two filters:
 Is it technically sound?
Do we understand the physics, engineering and risks? Do we have the necessary skills, tools and resources?
 Is it aligned with our values and policies?
Is it safe, compliant, ethical and positive for the long-term ecosystem we are trying to build?
 If either answer is “no”, we stop, rethink or redesign before moving forward.
