 Mission Objectives
 The Ekushey Rocket aims to gather critical atmospheric data and enhance our understanding of the Earth’s mesosphere. Equipped with advanced sensors, it will measure temperature, pressure, humidity, meteorological particles, and magnetic flux. The mission will support scientific research, improve weather forecasting, and contribute to climate change studies. Additionally, Ekushey will demonstrate next-generation communication systems, advancing data transmission technology for future space missions.
