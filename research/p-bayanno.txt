 Mission Objectives
 The Bayanno Rocket pioneers atmospheric research, testing communication systems and carrying a CANSAT payload. It honors the 1952 Bangla Language Movement, contributing to Bangladesh’s aerospace innovation and scientific discovery.
 Cansat 
 02
 Scientific Research
 The Rocket will deploy advanced sensors and instruments for atmospheric data collection, climate studies, and weather forecasting. With over 02 cutting-edge scientific tools, it will enhance environmental monitoring and climate change research.
 01
 student Cansat
 1 student CanSat satellites as payload, providing a unique opportunity for young engineers to contribute to space exploration. These CubeSats will offer valuable data for research, and educational purposes, promoting STEM engagement.
