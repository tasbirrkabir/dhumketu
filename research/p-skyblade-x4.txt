 Model: SkyBlade X4 VTOL Drone
 The SkyBlade X4 is a next-generation, 4-passenger VTOL human drone built for smart urban transportation and eco-friendly air travel. Powered by a cutting-edge hybrid hydrogen fuel cell-electric propulsion system, it delivers exceptional performance with zero emissions. The lightweight carbon-fiber construction and advanced AI flight systems ensure safety, stability, and efficiency. With a range of 250 km per charge and a cruising speed of 120 km/h, the SkyBlade X4 is ideal for city commutes, intercity travel, or leisure trips. Its sleek design, foldable rotor arms, and vertical take-off capabilities redefine the convenience and luxury of personal aerial transportation.
