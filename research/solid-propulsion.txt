 DhumketuX Aerospace has marked history with the development and testing of Bangladesh’s first indigenous rocket engines. From the humble Red Chili 01 to the powerful Agni Bina SRB, these engines are the heart of our suborbital rockets and sounding missions. Together, they symbolize the technical leap of a nation reaching for the stars.

 Red Chili Series
 Red Chili 01 | Red Chili 02
 Red Chili 01, tested in 2021, became Bangladesh’s first-ever rocket engine—an innovative solid-propellant motor designed for low-altitude science missions. Following its success, Red Chili 02 was developed with increased 5 kN thrust for stratospheric and educational payload launches.

 Proloy
 Power to Pierce the Sky
 Developed in 2023, Proloy 01 is a high-performance solid rocket booster engineered for Mesosphere missions. With over 15+ kN of thrust, it is capable of reaching the Mesosphere (25-50 km altitude), collecting vital atmospheric data for weather forecasting, ionospheric studies, and plasma science research.

 Agni Bina
 The Fire that Ignites the Future
 With a staggering 120+ kN thrust, Agni Bina is the powerhouse engine built for the Bidrohi rocket. This engine is a tribute to Bangladesh’s national poet Kazi Nazrul Islam and a symbol of self-reliant aerospace power. Agni Bina will carry the country’s first suborbital payloads to space.
