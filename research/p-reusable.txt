 Explore the Project
 Visionary Leap
 Revolation V1 is a visionary leap toward the future of reusable spaceflight. Developed by DhumketuX, this project enables autonomous rocket landings on solid ground, transforming rocket recovery systems and ensuring more sustainable space access.
 Traditional Recovery
 Traditional recovery methods, such as parachute-assisted splashdowns, involve complex logistics and expensive refurbishment. Revolation challenges these limitations by employing autonomous vertical landings using cutting-edge onboard intelligence
 Open Innovation
 The project is open-source and is released under the MIT License, allowing developers, educators, and space enthusiasts to contribute and accelerate collaborative advancements in aerospace technology.

 Mission Objectives
 Revolation V1 – Autonomous Reusable Rocket LandingsRevolation V1 by DhumketuX enables autonomous rocket landings on solid ground, transforming recovery systems for more sustainable space access. It overcomes parachute-assisted splashdowns, using cutting-edge onboard intelligence for precision landings.
Open Source InnovationReleased under the MIT License, Revolation V1 invites global collaboration from developers, educators, and space enthusiasts to advance aerospace technology.
 Reusable Rocket
 Autonomous GNC 
 Real-time onboard computing enables trajectory correction and descent stabilization, ensuring precision landings
 Precision Landing
 Rockets return safely to designated landing zones, significantly reducing recovery complexity, cost, time, and risk.
 Rapid Reusability
 Minimizes post-landing refurbishment, enabling faster mission cycles, cost savings, and improved reusability.
 Open Innovation
 Released under the MIT License, making it open to global collaboration with developers and aerospace enthusiasts
