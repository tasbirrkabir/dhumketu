 The Nazrul-01 SSLV is Bangladesh’s first Small Satellite Launch Vehicle, developed by DhumketuX Aerospace to launch small satellites into Low Earth Orbit (LEO). Named in honor of renowned physicist Jamal Nazrul Islam, it symbolizes a major leap in the nation's space journey and pays tribute to his lasting legacy in science and education.

 Mission Objectives
 At DhumketuX, our mission is to make space accessible, affordable, and sustainable. With the Nazrul-01 SSLV, we aim to position Bangladesh as a leader in space technology, offering efficient satellite launches and fostering scientific discovery, climate research, and STEM education. We’re committed to reducing launch costs and driving innovation for a brighter future in space.
